================================================================================================
SCRIPT BLIND SPOTS — known limitations
================================================================================================

The v2 sentence-extraction script captured 35 of these 48 YES suspects.
The remaining 13 were caught by a manual second-pass grep + reading. Constructions
that the v2 script doesn't reliably flag include:

  - 'Combined with the X credit and the Y credit, the payback period is...'
    (verb 'combined' not in POS_VERB_RE)
  - 'With IRA tax credits, the effective premium drops to $X.'
    (verb 'drops' on a different subject; the credit is in a prepositional phrase)
  - 'With tax credits, the payback drops to 3-5 years.'
    (same as above)
  - 'The 30% uncapped tax credit (25D) significantly reduces the cost gap.'
    (POS_VERB_RE has 'reduces? \w+ (by|to)' — too restrictive)
  - 'Factor in utility rebates ($200-$500) and the federal 25C tax credit (up to $600),
     and payback can drop to 3-6 years.'
    (verb 'factor in' / 'drop' not in regex; credit token is parenthetical)
  - 'We also cover the tax credits and rebates that can reduce your cost by 30-60%.'
    (verb 'cover' applies to the GUIDE not the credit; credit is the object)

Building a regex that catches ALL such constructions would be high-effort and probably
still incomplete; per user instruction ('stop chasing regex syntax'), the final word is
manual sentence-by-sentence reading. This list reflects that read.
