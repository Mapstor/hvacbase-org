# How to Drain a Portable AC

A portable air conditioner pulls moisture out of the room air as it cools. Many models get rid of most of that water by evaporating it into the exhaust air, but in humid weather the water collects faster than it evaporates, and the unit shuts itself off when its internal tank is full.

**To drain it, unplug the unit, place a shallow pan under the drain port at the back near the floor, remove the plug and let it empty, then replace the plug. If it fills often, connect a hose to the drain port for continuous gravity drainage to a floor drain, or use a condensate pump to lift the water out.**

## Why some portables rarely need draining

Many portables use the collected water to cool the condenser coil and send it out the exhaust hose as vapor. In dry weather, that keeps up with the water collected, and the unit may almost never need draining. In humid weather, or in dehumidify mode, it can't keep up, so the tank fills.

## Draining by hand

1. **Turn the unit off and unplug it.**
2. **Move it** to a spot where a little spilled water won't matter, or put a towel down.
3. **Place a shallow pan** under the lower drain port; portables sit low, so a baking sheet or shallow tray works better than a bucket.
4. **Remove the drain plug** and let the water drain; tilt the unit slightly toward the port to finish.
5. **Replace the plug firmly** before turning it back on.

Check the manual: some units have two ports, one for the condensate tank and one for continuous drainage in dehumidify mode.

## Continuous drainage

For humid weather, a hose on the drain port lets water run out continuously. It only works by gravity, so the hose has to run downhill all the way to a floor drain, a sink below the port's height, or outdoors, with no kinks or upward loops.

## Using a condensate pump

If there's no drain lower than the port, a small condensate pump collects the water and pumps it up and out to a sink or window. It needs an outlet, and its tubing must be secured where it discharges.

## How often to drain

It depends on humidity, room size and how long the unit runs. In humid weather a portable can fill in hours; in dry weather, rarely at all. The full-tank indicator and automatic shutoff tell you when.

## Preventing leaks and mold

- **Keep the drain plug tight** and the unit level.
- **Drain it fully before storing it**, then run the fan for a while or let it air dry with the plug out; standing water grows mold, which the EPA advises controlling by keeping things dry.
- **Don't drink or reuse the condensate**; it passes through the unit's coils and pan.
- **Clean the filter** regularly; a dirty filter can make the coil freeze and then drip as it thaws.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should I drain my portable AC?

Whenever the full-tank light comes on. In humid weather that may be every few hours; in dry weather it may rarely happen.

### Why does my portable AC fill up with water so fast?

High humidity, dehumidify mode, or a unit that doesn't evaporate its condensate. Continuous drainage solves it.

### Can I run a portable AC without draining it?

If it evaporates its condensate and the air is dry enough, often yes. Otherwise it will stop when the tank fills.

### Why is my portable AC leaking water?

Usually a loose drain plug, an uneven floor, a kinked drain hose, or a frozen coil from a dirty filter thawing.
