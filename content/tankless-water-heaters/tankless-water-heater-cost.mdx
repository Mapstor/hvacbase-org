# How Much Does a Tankless Water Heater Cost in 2026?

The price of a tankless water heater is mostly installation, not the unit. Swapping a tank for a tankless heater can mean a bigger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain, or for electric models, several new circuits and sometimes a new panel. Those extras decide whether the job lands near the bottom or the top of the range.

**Installed, a whole-house gas tankless heater typically runs $1,900 to $6,000, a whole-house electric one $1,150 to $3,100, and a small point-of-use electric unit $450 to $1,050.** These are typical ranges from contractor pricing, not quotes; get at least three itemized bids.

## Typical costs by type

| Type | Unit | Installation | Installed total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gas, non-condensing | $700 to $1,400 | $1,200 to $3,000 | $1,900 to $4,400 |
| Gas, condensing | $1,200 to $2,500 | $1,500 to $3,500 | $2,700 to $6,000 |
| Gas, outdoor (no indoor venting) | $900 to $2,200 | $1,000 to $2,500 | $1,900 to $4,700 |
| Electric, whole-house | $350 to $900 | $800 to $2,200 | $1,150 to $3,100 |
| Electric, point-of-use | $150 to $350 | $300 to $700 | $450 to $1,050 |

Condensing gas models cost more but extract more heat from the exhaust, and vent through PVC instead of stainless steel. Outdoor models skip indoor venting but need freeze protection in cold climates.

## What drives the installation price

| Item | Typical cost | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Gas line upgrade | up to about $800 | When the existing line can't supply the burner |
| New gas line run | up to about $1,200 | When the heater moves to a new spot |
| PVC venting (condensing) | $100 to $400 | Condensing gas models |
| Stainless venting (non-condensing) | $200 to $600 | Non-condensing gas models |
| Condensate drain | $50 to $200 | Condensing gas models |
| Electrician, new circuits | $300 to $800 | Electric models, before panel work |
| Panel upgrade | $1,500 to $4,000 | When the panel can't carry the load |
| Permits | $50 to $200 | Most jurisdictions |

Whole-house electric units are the ones that surprise people. A 27 kW model draws about 112 amps at 240 volts, which many 100- or 125-amp panels can't spare; see our [panel upgrade cost guide](/electrical-panel-upgrade-cost) and [tankless sizing guide](/what-size-tankless-water-heater).

## Running cost and payback

Using our [water heater sizing calculator](/water-heater-sizing-calculator)'s default four-person household, at $1.35 per therm and 18 cents per kWh:

| Type | Energy cost per year |
|---|---|
| Heat pump water heater | $334 |
| Tankless, gas | $340 |
| Tank, gas | $467 |
| Tankless, electric | $1,193 |
| Tank, electric | $1,271 |

A gas tankless heater saves about $127 a year over a gas tank, mostly by not keeping a tank of water hot around the clock. If it costs $1,500 more to install, that takes about 12 years to pay back. Electric tankless saves only about $78 a year over an electric tank, while a heat pump water heater saves about $937, which is why the heat pump is usually the better electric upgrade.

## Tax credits and rebates in 2026

- **The federal 25C credit ended** for water heaters placed in service after December 31, 2025. See [HVAC tax credits 2026](/hvac-tax-credits-2026).
- **HEAR rebates cover heat pump water heaters** (up to $1,750 for eligible households), not gas or electric tankless heaters.
- **Utility rebates vary.** Some gas utilities pay for high-efficiency tankless units; the DSIRE database lists programs by state and utility.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to install a tankless water heater?

For a whole-house gas unit, typically $1,900 to $6,000 including the unit, depending on venting and gas line work. Whole-house electric runs $1,150 to $3,100 before any panel upgrade.

### Is a tankless water heater worth the cost?

A gas tankless heater saves about $127 a year over a gas tank for a typical four-person household, so a $1,500 premium takes about 12 years to recover. It makes sense mainly if you value endless hot water or need the space.

### Why is electric tankless installation so expensive?

Whole-house units need very large circuits, often more than an older panel can supply. The panel upgrade, not the heater, is usually the big cost.

### Is there a tax credit for tankless water heaters in 2026?

No. The federal credit ended after 2025, and HEAR rebates cover heat pump water heaters, not tankless units. Check your utility for local rebates.
