## #1 Mitsubishi (FX-Series 12K) — Best Cooling Efficiency

Mitsubishi Electric has manufactured ductless systems longer than any brand currently selling in North America. The current top single-zone is the FX-Series, on the new R-454B refrigerant that meets the EPA's 2026 GWP-700 install threshold.

| Spec | Value | Source |
|------|-------|--------|
| SEER2 (12K) | **29.9** | hvacdirect Mitsubishi FX-Series 12K listing |
| Refrigerant | **R-454B** | hvacdirect Mitsubishi FX-Series 12K listing |
| Min heating temp | **-13°F (H2i Hyper-Heat)** | hvacdirect Mitsubishi FX-Series 12K listing |
| Warranty (Diamond Contractor install) | 12 years | Mitsubishi warranty terms |
| AHRI cert | {{PENDING-AHRI}} (Marko to pull) | — |

**Heat-transfer angle (why high SEER2 = high real-world efficiency):** SEER2 is a weighted seasonal metric — most of the score comes from how the unit performs at *part load*, not full blast. A variable-speed inverter compressor that can hold a steady low output (20–40% of capacity) for hours saves enormous compressor-start-up energy versus a unit that cycles on and off. 29.9 SEER2 reflects an inverter that modulates fine enough to almost never cycle in shoulder-season conditions.

**Companion line — Mitsubishi MSZ-FS12NA (the unit previously claimed at "40.1 SEER2"):** real values per the gotductless / Ferguson submittals are **26.3 SEER2, 13.8 EER2, 10.4–11.1 HSPF2** (depending on listing), R-410A on sold-through stock. The 40.1 SEER2 claim was a fabrication and has been removed sitewide.

## #2 Daikin (Aurora 12K) — Best Warranty + Cold Climate

Daikin is the world's largest HVAC manufacturer by revenue. The Aurora 12K (FTXV12AVJU9 / RXT12AVJU9) is the single SKU we have a primary-source spec sheet for in this batch.

| Spec | Value | Source |
|------|-------|--------|

| EER2 | **12.0** | totalhomesupply manufacturer spec |

| Warranty (registered) | **12 years all parts** | Daikin Comfort Pro terms |
| AHRI cert | Pending pull (cited as #215710688 — confirm via AHRI Directory) | — |

**Heat-transfer angle (why H2i / EVI maintains capacity in cold):** at outdoor temperatures below ~25°F, refrigerant arriving at the compressor inlet is at very low density, and a stock compressor can't move enough mass to deliver useful heat. **Enhanced vapor injection (EVI) / flash injection** pulls a side stream of partially-condensed refrigerant back into a mid-stage port on the compressor — increasing the mass flow rate without changing the compressor's physical displacement. That's what lets the Aurora deliver 100% rated heating capacity at 5°F and still operate at -13°F.

**Heat-transfer angle (why a budget unit can still hit 21+ SEER2):** modern variable-speed inverter compressors are no longer the cost frontier they were a decade ago. The compressor castings, EEV control boards, and BLDC fan motors that make 21 SEER2 possible are now widely available across the supply chain. The trade-offs at this price tier are **build refinement** (heavier-gauge copper, tighter refrigerant-charge tolerances, polished controls software) and **long-term reliability** — not the headline SEER2 number.

## Refrigerant Transition (2025–2026 AIM Act)

Critical context for any 2026 purchase decision:

1. **Manufacturing cutoff: Jan 1, 2025.** Manufacturers may no longer produce or import new residential split-system AC or heat pumps using R-410A. Equipment now ships with **R-454B (GWP 466)** or **R-32 (GWP 675)** — both A2L, both under the EPA's 700-GWP threshold.
2. **Installation: R-410A is not banned.** Equipment manufactured before Jan 1, 2025 may still be installed (EPA amended final rule, effective July 27, 2026) until existing inventory depletes. Existing systems can be serviced and recharged indefinitely.
3. **State caveat:** Some states are stricter. **New York codified the original Jan 1, 2026 install ban.** Check your state and local code.

(Sources: EPA Technology Transitions rule; NAHB May 2026 on the amended install rule; ACDirect R-410A phase-out timeline; ICC Q4-2025 update.)

For the three shortlisted units: Mitsubishi FX-Series is **R-454B**; Daikin Aurora is **R-32**; Senville LETO is **R-454B**. The older Mitsubishi MSZ-FS line (the unit previously claimed at "40.1 SEER2") still ships with **R-410A** stock through existing inventory.
