# Best Mini Split Line Set Cover Kits (DIY Installation Guide)

Line set covers are decorative PVC or metal channels that conceal the refrigerant lines, condensate drain, and communication wire running between your mini split's indoor and outdoor units. The best overall cover kit is the Decorative PVC Line Cover Kit (Rectorseal LD series or equivalent) at $80–$150 for a complete installation — paintable, UV-resistant, and available in straight sections, 90° elbows, couplings, and wall entries to handle any routing.

<TableOfContents />

## Why Install Line Set Covers?

Without covers, your mini split's connection bundle (two copper lines wrapped in black insulation, a drain line, and a wire) runs exposed along your exterior wall. While functional, exposed lines look messy, are vulnerable to UV degradation, and can be damaged by lawn equipment, animals, or weather.

| Factor | Without Covers | With Covers |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Aesthetics | Exposed black insulation | Clean, paintable channel |
| UV protection | Insulation degrades in 3–5 years | Protected indefinitely |
| Physical protection | Vulnerable to impact | Shielded by rigid PVC |
| Pest protection | Rodents can chew insulation | Enclosed and sealed |
| HOA compliance | Often fails HOA standards | Typically approved |
| Cost | $0 | $80–$300 |

## Line-Set Cover Types

Line-set covers come in three material families, and the choice affects both install method and durability.

**Rigid PVC ductwork** is the most common. Sold as pre-cut straight lengths (typically 78" or 84") with matching corner elbows, tee fittings, wall inlets, and end caps. PVC covers snap onto a mounting base fastened to the wall first — the line set drops in from above, then the cover snaps over. Paintable with any exterior-grade acrylic latex. Ships in white or grey; premium lines offer several colors. RectorSeal Slimduct (widely stocked at HVAC supply houses) and Diversitech DiaCover are the two most common brands.

**Aluminum snap-lock covers** cost more but resist UV and impact better. Same install method (mount base, snap cover). Preferred for exposed south/west walls where PVC yellows over 5–10 years.

**Molded ducted trim (paintable, seamless)** — Fortress LineSyGuard and similar products come as pre-finished paintable covers with better joinery at the corners. More expensive than base-tier PVC, but the finished look is closer to permanent trim than "AC accessory."

For unfinished garages, sheds, or utility applications, plain white PVC electrical conduit (2" or 3" schedule 40) is a low-cost alternative — mechanically similar to purpose-built cover, minus the snap-lock convenience.

## Sizing the Cover to Your Line Set

Cover width must fit the line-set outer diameter plus the condensate drain and low-voltage communication cable, which run alongside the copper.

| Mini-split BTU | Typical liquid line | Typical suction line | Recommended cover width |
| ---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 9,000 | 1/4" OD | 3/8" OD | 3" (75 mm) |
| 12,000 | 1/4" OD | 3/8" or 1/2" OD | 3" or 3.5" (75–90 mm) |
| 18,000 | 3/8" OD | 1/2" OD | 3.5" (90 mm) |
| 24,000 | 3/8" OD | 5/8" OD | 4.5" (100 mm) |
| 36,000+ | 3/8" OD | 3/4" OD | 4.5" (100 mm) or 5.5" (140 mm) for multi-zone bundles |

If the outdoor run bundles two or more zone line sets from a multi-zone system, size up one width class — a 3-zone system's three parallel line sets typically need a 5.5" cover even if each individual set would fit a smaller one.

## Install Steps (Snap-Lock PVC or Aluminum)

All manufacturers publish install manuals; the sequence below is the general order of operations across brands.

1. **Route-plan first.** Sketch the run from indoor unit to outdoor unit. Minimize elbows — each 90° elbow adds refrigerant pressure drop and slows install. Prefer one or two large-radius turns over many short ones. Keep the cover at least 4" clear of window trim and door frames.
2. **Mount the base rail.** Level the mounting base against the wall in 2–3 sections. Use exterior-grade screws sized for your wall material (wood siding: #10 × 2" stainless; brick or stucco: ¼" × 1-¾" concrete screws; vinyl siding: pre-drill and use wide-thread siding screws to avoid cracking).
3. **Cut cover to length.** A fine-tooth hacksaw or PVC pipe cutter produces the cleanest edge. Deburr with a utility knife. For aluminum, use a metal-cutting blade in a jigsaw.
4. **Test-fit elbows and couplings dry** before running the line set. Corner fittings snap onto adjacent straight sections and must align with the mounting base.
5. **Route the bundle through the mounting channel.** The refrigerant lines, condensate drain, and low-voltage cable run parallel inside the cover. Insulate refrigerant lines separately (3/4-inch closed-cell insulation on the suction line at minimum — see the [cold-weather heat pump guide](/heat-pump-in-cold-weather/) if this run is exposed to freezing temps).
6. **Seal the wall penetration.** The through-wall port needs a dedicated wall-collar (included with most Slimduct-tier kits) plus exterior-grade silicone or urethane sealant on the outside. Interior-side, use vapor-permeable sealant to allow drying.
7. **Snap the cover on.** Start at one end and work toward the other, seating each section on the base rail. Corner elbows and couplings snap into place last.
8. **Paint if desired.** Clean the cover, prime with a plastic-adhering primer for PVC (or self-etching primer for aluminum), then apply exterior acrylic latex. Two thin coats hold up better than one thick.

### Brand Fitment Notes

- **RectorSeal Slimduct** — 3", 3.5", 4.5" widths. Snap-lock PVC. Widest accessory ecosystem (wall inlets, decorative trim, condensate drain adapters). Compatible with essentially every mini-split brand.
- **Diversitech DiaCover** — 3", 3.5", 4.5", 5.5" widths. Direct competitor to Slimduct at similar price point. Same install method.
- **Fortress LineSyGuard** — 3.5" and 4.5". Molded corners, cleaner finished look. Higher price. Compatible with any brand's line set.
- **MrCool universal covers** — sized for MrCool DIY line sets specifically; use the manufacturer-matched kit if installing a MrCool DIY system.

Before ordering, measure your line-set outer diameter with a caliper or wrap a paper strip around it (circumference ÷ 3.14 = OD). The rated cover width must exceed the combined bundle diameter by at least 25% to allow slack for expansion and condensate drain routing.

- **Slimduct and DiaCover fit any brand; MrCool DIY needs its manufacturer-matched cover.** RectorSeal Slimduct (3"/3.5"/4.5" widths) and Diversitech DiaCover (3"–5.5") are snap-lock PVC that work with essentially every mini-split brand's line set — MrCool universal covers, by contrast, are sized specifically for MrCool DIY line sets, so use the OEM kit on those systems.
